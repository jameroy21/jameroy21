Privacy

Clear Price keeps nothing. Here is exactly what happens.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no cookies, no advertising and no analytics that identify you.

What happens to the numbers you type

The price and discounts you type are sent to our calculation service to work out the answer, and are used for that one request only. They are never written to a database, never logged with your details and never shared or sold. Nothing is stored on our side, so there is nothing to delete.

If your phone has no signal, the calculation happens on your phone instead and nothing is sent anywhere.

What our servers see

Like every website, our host records basic technical information when a page is requested, such as your IP address and the time. This is used to keep the service running and to stop abuse. The calculation itself deliberately does not record the numbers you typed.

Permissions

Clear Price asks for no camera, location, microphone, contacts or payment access. If you install it on your phone, it stores the app's own files so it opens quickly and works offline — nothing more.

Children

Clear Price is a simple calculator, suitable for all ages, and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on the project's GitHub repository, or email the address listed on the project profile.
