Privacy

No accounts, no cookies, no personal data — and the prices you type are never stored. Here is exactly what happens.

What is collected

Two things, both anonymous: that the app was installed on a device, and that it was opened on a given day. These come with a random id created on your phone, so the app can count distinct devices without knowing whose they are. It tells us "40 people installed it, 12 came back yesterday" — and nothing else.

There is no account, no sign-up, no email address, no phone number, no password, no cookie, no advertising identifier, and no third-party analytics. Your IP address is never stored.

Your choice, one tap

Where anonymous counting is switched on, the footer of the app shows Anonymous counts: On / Off. Switch it off and nothing is ever sent again from that device. (Some deployments — for example a copy hosted as plain static files — never count anything at all, and then the switch is not shown.) The app also respects your browser's Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control settings automatically — if they ask us not to count you, we do not. Clearing your browser data makes the app forget the random id, so your next visit looks brand new.

What happens to the numbers you type

The price and discounts you type are sent to our calculation service to work out the answer, and are used for that one request only. They are never written to a database, never logged with your details and never shared or sold. Nothing is stored on our side, so there is nothing to delete.

If your phone has no signal, the calculation happens on your phone instead and nothing is sent anywhere.

What our servers see

Like every website, our host records basic technical information when a page is requested, such as the IP address and the time. This is used to keep the service running and to stop abuse (for example, limiting how many requests one address can make). The calculation itself deliberately does not record the numbers you typed, and the anonymous counters do not include IP addresses at all.

Permissions

Clear Price asks for no camera, location, microphone, contacts or payment access. If you install it on your phone, it stores the app's own files so it opens quickly and works offline — nothing more.

Children

Clear Price is a simple calculator, suitable for all ages, and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

How long the counts are kept

Anonymous daily counts are deleted after about 13 months. Because there is no database of people, there is nothing else to delete.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on the project's GitHub repository, or email the address listed on the project profile. See also our terms of use.
